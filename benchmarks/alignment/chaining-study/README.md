# Chaining experiment — 4 November 2026

Isolated experiment against faqt ef236e24be64ff7296290faadb2320bdc951bead.
Production faqt and the Seqhiker backend were not changed.

## Diagnosis

The reported nested reverse-strand alignments are generated by alternative
chains in the CP026802.1-to-CP151312.1 search. The chains share 49 anchors.
The shorter chain ends with a different 476 bp exact anchor; the longer one
continues through three other anchors. Refinement splits both chains before
this region. The opposite search direction also generates the longer result.
Thus changing extraction in only one search direction would be insufficient.

## Experiment

Extract the highest-scoring leaf chain first, then stop subsequent backtracking
at an anchor already claimed by another chain. Preserve unused branches.
All comparisons use adaptive extension and the existing Near reporting policy;
its 99% threshold and 100 bp exception were not changed.

Variants:

- `baseline`: existing complete backtracking from every leaf.
- `leaves`: consume shared anchors, rescore unused branches.
- `all`: same, considering all endpoints rather than leaves alone.
- `validated`: split candidates at exact anchors already represented by accepted
  alignments, rather than claiming anchors before refinement.
- `boundary`: preserve one shared boundary anchor, but require the unused branch
  alone to satisfy the minimum chain score.
- `boundary-score`: preserve one boundary anchor and add its exact-match length
  to the residual branch score for short-branch rescue. This deliberately omits
  the boundary transition penalty from eligibility scoring.
- `boundary-rescore`: retain the boundary anchor and apply the full transition
  penalty when rescoring the resulting short chain.

## Results

A large hit here has at least 10,000 paired positions. Containment counts require
both coordinate ranges to be enclosed by another large hit on the same contig
pair and strand. Paired retention measures the union of distinct strand-specific
aligned base pairs relative to baseline; it is not a biological accuracy score.

| Variant | Original large contained hits | Repeats large contained hits | Original paired retention | Repeats paired retention |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | 2 | 38 | 100% | 100% |
| Leaves | 0 | 0 | 98.8292% | 99.9783% |
| All endpoints | 0 | 0 | 98.7584% | 99.9739% |
| Validated | 0 | 0 | 99.3210% | 99.9779% |
| Boundary | 1 | 2 | 98.8706% | 99.9932% |
| Boundary + short-branch rescue | 1 | 2 | 99.9274% | 99.9997% |
| Boundary + full rescore | 1 | 2 | 99.6221% | 99.9985% |

The three boundary variants preserve the entire baseline union of covered
sequence intervals on both genomes in both datasets. The simpler variants lose
451–490 covered bases on repeat genome A and 70–138 on repeat genome B.

For the reported pair, the boundary variants retain the 69 kb alignment and
replace the nested 58 kb alternative with a 2,280 bp local alternative. That
short path retains all 105 base pairings absent from the larger path. It remains
contained in the larger coordinate rectangle. Chaining therefore reduces the
large duplicated backbone but does not establish a rule against all contained
results. Distinct repeat placements can legitimately overlap in coordinates.

The most sensitive boundary variant produces 6,635 original / 40,107 repeat
paths, versus baseline 6,609 / 39,933: large overlaps decrease, but short local
candidates increase. It loses 2,243 previous distinct pairings and gains 3,549
on the original data; on repeats it loses 102 and gains 5,392.

Three serial, alternating runs with GOMAXPROCS=1, macOS arm64 SIMD:

| Dataset | Baseline median alignment time | Boundary + short-branch rescue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original | 3.148 s | 3.263 s |
| Repeats | 10.977 s | 11.305 s |

No speed benefit demonstrated. These times exclude preparation and PAF writing.
Every repeated PAF was byte-identical to its variant's initial PAF.

## Validation and conclusion

All fourteen dataset/variant PAFs replay against the original DNA. The full
SIMD Go suite passes with the short-branch rescue variant, including the opt-in
original missing-hit and input-swap regressions. Scalar alignment tests pass.
The fully rescored boundary variant also passes alignment tests and the real-data
regressions. Synthetic checks retain the 100 kb main diagonal plus the embedded
1 kb off-diagonal repeats, Cartesian repeat copies, and tandem-copy placements.

Greedy extraction is promising for removing long duplicated chain prefixes.
Aggressive clipping also loses valid short alternative alignments; preserving
boundary context mitigates this. It does not guarantee non-overlapping final
alignments, and it is not a measured speed improvement. Keep this as an experiment
until the short-branch eligibility and final reporting policy are chosen.
